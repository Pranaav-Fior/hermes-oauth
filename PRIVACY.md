# Hermes — privacy policy

Hermes is a personal-use application operated by its owner for their own Google Account.

- **What it accesses.** Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Docs, solely to read and edit
  documents that the operating user owns or that are shared with them.
- **Where data goes.** Nowhere else. No Google content is transmitted to third parties and no
  copy of it is retained beyond the operator's own server, where the OAuth token is kept so the
  application can act on the operator's behalf.
- **Third parties.** None. There is no analytics, advertising or data sharing.
- **Revoking access.** Access can be revoked at any time at
  https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored token.
- **Contact.** mallipran@gmail.com
